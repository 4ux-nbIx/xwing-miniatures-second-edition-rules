Rules Reference

SPEED

Each maneuver has three components: speed (a number 0–5), difficulty (red, white, or blue), and bearing (an arrow or other symbol).

  • If the speed of a maneuver is increased or decreased, the speed of the maneuver is restricted to the templates that exist.

    • For example, the speed of a [3 ] cannot be increased and the speed of a [1 ] cannot be decreased.

  • The speed of a [0 ] cannot be increased or decreased.

  • Even if a ship partially executes a maneuver, it is still treated as having executed a maneuver of the indicated speed.
